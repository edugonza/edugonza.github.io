I'm Eduardo, a machine learning engineer at [Xebia](https://xebia.com) in Eindhoven, the
Netherlands. I have a PhD in process mining, a background in full stack development and 
I like solving challenging problems that sit between research and production.

## Background

I joined [Xebia](https://xebia.com) in 2026. Before that, I spent eight years as a full stack developer and
machine learning engineer at Precedence (now [Sia](https://www.sia-partners.com)).

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from Eindhoven University of Technology (2014–2019).
My thesis, [*Process Mining on Databases: Extracting Event Data from Real-Life Data
Sources*](https://research.tue.nl/p/en/research-outputs/process-mining-on-databases-extracting-event-data-from-real-life-), is about getting event data out of real systems, where many business
processes coexist and domain knowledge is often missing, and about querying that data
to build better event logs for process mining.

Before that I was a researcher at the [University of Zaragoza](https://www.unizar.es). I worked at GIDHE (Distributed and Heterogeneous Systems Integration Group), in
the [Department of Computer Science and Systems Engineering](https://diis.unizar.es), and before that at
Tecnodiscap, building technology for people with disabilities and the elderly. I
studied Computer Science Engineering and did a master's in Computing and Systems
Engineering in Zaragoza, with an Erasmus year at [Tampere University of Technology](https://www.tuni.fi) in
Finland.
